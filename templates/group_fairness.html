What does this result mean?

Selection Rate Ratio: Compares a group's approval rate to the highest-approved group (with at least 100 applicants where available). A ratio of 0.75 means this group is approved at 75% of the reference group's rate.

Fisher Exact Test p-value: Measures whether the approval gap could plausibly happen by random chance. A small p-value (< 0.05) suggests the disparity is statistically real rather than noise.

Equal Opportunity Gap (TPR Gap): Among applicants who actually repaid their loan (approved = 1), how much lower is this group's approval rate compared to the best group? A large gap means qualified applicants in this group are rejected more often.

Why groups with n < 30 show a warning: Small groups (like 37 foreign workers or tiny age brackets) produce unstable percentages. RGCA flags small sample sizes so you do not build a legal claim on noisy counts.
